## Overview

Optivus is a modern CRM platform designed to provide a scalable and maintainable foundation for managing customer relationships and business operations.

Built around a microservices architecture, the platform focuses on reliability, flexibility, and long-term scalability while enabling independent service development and deployment.

The project is currently under active development and continues to evolve as new business requirements and architectural improvements are introduced.

---

## Vision

Our vision is to build a flexible CRM ecosystem that enables organizations to adapt, scale, and optimize their workflows without being constrained by monolithic architectures.

---

## Architecture

Optivus follows a distributed architecture designed around independent services, clear domain boundaries, and specialized data storage solutions.

Core technologies currently used within the platform include:

- TypeScript
- NestJS
- PostgreSQL
- MongoDB
- Redis
